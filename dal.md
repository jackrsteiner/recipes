# Red Lentil Curry
*Serves: 4*

[Link](https://rainbowplantlife.com/vegan-red-lentil-curry/) ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHRyfEbhFFU)), an ultra-creamy, Indian-inspired red lentil curry by Nisha Vora of Rainbow Plant Life. Made in one pot with pantry staples in about 45 minutes.

## Ingredients
| Ingredient | Preparation | Quantity | Unit |
| ---------- | ----------- | -------- | ---- |
| Coconut oil | --- | 1 | tbsp |
| Garlic | minced | 4 | cloves |
| Ginger | peeled and minced | 2 | inches |
| Turmeric | ground | 1 | tsp |
| Serrano peppers | diced | 1–2 | --- |
| Cumin | ground | 1 | tsp |
| Coriander | ground | 1/2 | tsp |
| Chili powder | Indian red | 1/2 | tsp |
| Curry powder | --- | 2 | tsp |
| Garam masala | --- | 1 | tsp |
| Salt | --- | 1 | tsp |
| Pepper | ground | to taste | --- |
| Red lentils | rinsed | 1 | cup |
| Vegetable broth | low-sodium | 2 | cups |
| Tomatoes | crushed | 1 | can |
| Coconut cream | --- | 1 | can |
| Almond butter | unsweetened, creamy | 3 | tbsp |
| Lemon | juiced | 1/2 | --- |
| Cilantro | chopped | 1/2 | cup |
| Rice or flatbread | for serving | --- | --- |

## Steps
1. Rinse 1 cup **red lentils** in cold water until the water runs clear.
2. Heat 1 tbsp **coconut oil** in a large, deep skillet or saucepan over medium-high heat. Once the oil is shimmering, add 4 cloves minced **garlic**, 2 inches minced **ginger**, and 1–2 diced **serrano peppers**. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring often so the garlic doesn't burn.
3. Add 1 tsp **turmeric**, 1 tsp **cumin**, 1/2 tsp **coriander**, 1/2 tsp **chili powder**, 2 tsp **curry powder**, 1 tsp **garam masala**, 1 tsp **salt**, and **pepper** to taste. Cook for 30–60 seconds until fragrant, stirring often.
4. Pour in 2 cups **vegetable broth** and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Add the lentils and 1 can crushed **tomatoes** and mix well. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20–25 minutes, until the lentils are cooked through and mostly soft. If they're not soft yet, add a few spoons of broth or water and cook 5 minutes more.
5. Uncover and stir in 1 can **coconut cream** and 3 tbsp **almond butter**, plus salt and pepper to taste. Cook on low heat, uncovered, for 5–8 minutes, until thick and creamy.
6. Stir in the juice of 1/2 **lemon** and 1/2 cup chopped **cilantro**, then turn off the heat. For a creamier, more puréed curry, blend lightly with an immersion blender.
7. Serve with **rice or flatbread** and garnish with more cilantro. Leftovers keep in the fridge for 3–4 days.

*Spice level: leave out the chili peppers for no heat. For mild heat, use 1 serrano with the seeds removed. For a spicy curry, use 2 serranos with the seeds left in.*
