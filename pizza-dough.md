# Pizza Dough
*Serves: 2 bases*

## Ingredients
| Ingredient | Preparation | Quantity | Unit |
| ---------- | ----------- | -------- | ---- |
| Flour | plain or bread | 500 | g |
| Water | warm | 325 | g |
| Instant Dry Yeast | --- | 7 | g (about 2¼ tsp) |
| Salt | --- | 10 | g |
| Honey | --- | 15 | g (about 2 tsp) |
| Olive Oil | --- | 15 | g (about 1 tbsp) |

## Steps
1. Mix the **warm water**, **honey**, and **yeast**. Leave for 5–10 minutes until the yeast becomes foamy.
2. Stir the **olive oil** into the yeast mixture.
3. In a separate bowl, mix the **salt** into the **flour**.
4. Add the wet mixture to the flour and mix until a rough dough forms.
5. Knead for 7–10 minutes, until smooth and elastic.
6. Cover and let rise somewhere warm for 1–1½ hours, until roughly doubled.
7. Divide into 2 equal balls. Cover and rest for 30–45 minutes.
8. Stretch each ball into a pizza base and add toppings.
9. Bake in a fully preheated oven at its maximum temperature, ideally 250–300°C, on a hot tray, stone, or steel for about 5–10 minutes.
