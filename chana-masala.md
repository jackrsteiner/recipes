# Easy Chana Masala
*Serves: 4*

Created with the help of ChatGPT.

## Ingredients
| Ingredient | Preparation | Quantity | Unit |
| ---------- | ----------- | -------- | ---- |
| Chickpeas | drained and rinsed | 1 | can |
| Onion | chopped | 1 | ---- |
| Tomatoes | crushed | 1 | can |
| Garlic | minced | 3 | cloves |
| Ginger | minced | 1 | inch |
| Green chili | minced | 1 | ---- |
| Cumin seeds | ---- | 1 | tsp |
| Turmeric | powder | 1 | tsp |
| Coriander | powder | 1 | tsp |
| Cumin | powder | 1 | tsp |
| Paprika | powder | 1 | tsp |
| Garam masala | powder | 1 | tsp |
| Salt | ---- | 1 | tsp |
| Vegetable oil | ---- | 2 | tsp |
| Water | ---- | 1 | cup |
| Cilantro | leaves | 1 | bunch |
| Lemon | juiced | 1 | ---- |

## Steps
1. Heat the **vegetable oil** (2 tsp) in a large pan over medium heat.
2. Add the **cumin seeds** (1 tsp) and let them sizzle for a few seconds until aromatic.
3. Add the chopped **onion** (1) and sauté until golden brown.
4. Add the minced **garlic** (3 cloves), **ginger** (1 inch), and **green chili** (1). Sauté for another 2-3 minutes.
5. Add the **turmeric** (1 tsp), **coriander** (1 tsp), **cumin** powder (1 tsp), and **paprika** (1 tsp). Stir well and cook for another minute to let the spices bloom.
6. Add the crushed **tomatoes** (1 can) and cook for about 5-7 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld.
7. Add the **chickpeas** (1 can, drained and rinsed), stirring to coat them in the spice mixture.
8. Add the **water** (1 cup) and bring to a simmer. Cook for about 10-15 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken.
9. Add the **garam masala** (1 tsp) and **salt** (1 tsp), stirring well to combine.
10. Garnish with fresh **cilantro** leaves (1 bunch) and the juice of the **lemon** (1).
