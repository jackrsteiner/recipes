# Bean Burrito
[Vegetarian Bean and Rice Burrito](https://www.thespruceeats.com/vegetarian-bean-and-rice-burrito-recipe-3378550) on thespruceeats.com

## Ingredients
| Ingredient | Preparation | Quantity |
| ---------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Rice | rinse | 4 cups |
| Cilantro | chop | 4 tbsp |
| Lime | juice | 2 |
| Tortillas | --- | 6 large |
| Onion | dice | 1 medium |
| Garlic | mince | 8 cloves |
| Olive oil | --- | 3 tbsp |
| Black beans | drain | 2 cans |
| Chilli | powder | 2 tbsp |
| Cumin | ground | 1 tsp |
| Salt | --- | 1 pinch |

## Steps
1. Cook 4 cups **rice**.
2. Toss **rice**, 4 tbsp **cilantro**, and juice from 2 **limes**.
3. Saute 1 medium **onion** 5 minutes until soft in 3 tbsp **olive oil**.
4. Add 8 cloves **garlic** to onion and cook additional minute.
5. Reduce heat to medium low.
6. Add 2 cans **beans** and combine with 2 tbsp **chili powder**, 1 tsp **cumin**, and 1 pinch **salt**.
7. Cook addtional 5 minutes.
