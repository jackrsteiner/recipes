# Bean Burrito
[Vegetarian Bean and Rice Burrito](https://www.thespruceeats.com/vegetarian-bean-and-rice-burrito-recipe-3378550) on thespruceeats.com

## Ingredients
| Ingredient | Preparation | Quantity |
| ---------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Rice | rinse | 4 cups |
| Cilantro | chop | 4 tbsp |
| Lime | juice | 2 |
| Tortillas | --- | 6 large |
| Olive oil | --- | 1 tbsp |
| Onion | finely dice | 1 small |
| Garlic | mince | 2–3 cloves |
| Cumin | ground | 1 tsp |
| Smoked paprika | --- | 1 tsp |
| Oregano | dried | ½ tsp |
| Chili powder | --- | ½ tsp |
| Passata | --- | ½ cup |
| Black or pinto beans | drain and rinse | 2 × 400 g cans |
| Salt | --- | ½ tsp |
| Black pepper | --- | ¼ tsp |
| Water | --- | ¼ cup |
| Lime juice or vinegar | --- | 1 tsp |
| Sugar or honey (optional) | --- | ½ tsp |

## Steps
1. Cook **rice**.
2. Toss **rice**, **cilantro**, and juice from **limes**.
3. Heat **olive oil** in a frying pan over medium heat. Add **onion** and cook for 5–7 minutes until soft and starting to brown.
4. Add **garlic**, **cumin**, **smoked paprika**, **oregano**, and **chili powder**. Cook for about 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
5. Add **passata** and cook for 3–4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens and darkens slightly.
6. Add **beans**, **salt**, **black pepper**, and **water**. Simmer for 8–10 minutes.
7. Mash about half to two-thirds of the **beans** directly in the pan. Continue cooking until the mixture is thick and cohesive.
8. Stir in **lime juice or vinegar**. If the tomato tastes too acidic, add **sugar or honey**.
